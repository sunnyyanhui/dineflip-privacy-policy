Privacy Policy

App: Dine Flip  ·  Last updated: 23 October 2026

Dine Flip ("the app") is developed by My Lovely Angel. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data required to provide the app's features:

  • Device location — collected via Apple's CoreLocation framework when you use Dining Out mode. Location is used solely to find nearby restaurants and is never stored on our servers.

We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Payment card details or financial information of any kind
  • Persistent device identifiers or advertising IDs

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Location + search queries are sent to the Google Places API (operated by Google LLC) to return a list of nearby restaurants. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Free trial state (start date and duration) is stored only in Apple's UserDefaults on your device. It is never transmitted off-device.

3. Subscription Payments

Dine Flip Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store and StoreKit. We never receive, see, or store your payment card details. All billing is handled by Apple and is subject to Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.

4. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Disclosure

The app may display links to third-party food delivery services (currently UberEats). Tapping these links opens the third party's website or app; their own privacy policies apply.

We participate in the AWIN affiliate programme. In the future, some delivery links may be tracked affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you place an order at no extra cost to you. At the time of this policy, all affiliate merchant IDs are inactive (set to 0) and no commission tracking is in use. This notice will be updated when affiliate tracking becomes active.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes. The only third-party service that receives data is Google Places API (see Section 2), and only for the purpose of providing in-app restaurant search results.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store any personal data on our servers, there is nothing to delete on our end. Trial state stored in UserDefaults can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Dine Flip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
